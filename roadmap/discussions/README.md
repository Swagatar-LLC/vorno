# Discussions

Captured thinking. Conversations, dossiers, research notes, brainstorm logs. Discussions are **never authoritative** — they exist for plans and decisions to draw from.

## When to capture

- A meaningful conversation with a collaborator (human or agent) that shifted thinking.
- A research dossier or external survey we want to keep grounded in the repo.
- A brainstorm session whose ideas feed multiple plans.
- A "rabbit hole" exploration that may not become a plan immediately but is worth not losing.

## Format

`YYYY-MM-DD-short-kebab-title.md` — date prefix for chronological sort.

Recommended frontmatter:

```yaml
---
date: 2026-04-28
participants: [jh, claude-opus]
topic: Canvas paradigm directions
related-decisions: [ADR-0003]
related-directions: [DIR-01, DIR-02, DIR-03]
---
```
